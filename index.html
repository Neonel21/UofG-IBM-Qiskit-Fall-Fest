University of Glasgow IBM Qiskit Fall Fest 2026

Welcome to the Qiskit Fall Fest at the UofG! We're thrilled to have you here. Qiskit Fall Fest is an international series of quantum computing events across university campuses, supported and endorsed by IBM Quantum.

Join us for an immersive workshop co-hosted by IBM Quantum, where early discovery, skill development, and collaboration are prioritized. Dive into quantum algorithms, circuit design, hands-on labs, expert keynotes, and networking opportunities with pioneers across academia and industry!

Speakers to be Announced Soon

We are putting the finishing touches on an inspiring lineup featuring leading quantum scientists, IBM Quantum specialists, and industry trailblazers. Full bios, keynote topics, and talk schedules will be unveiled shortly!

Register for Event
Prof. Martin Weides

Chair of Quantum Technologies (Electronic & Nanoscale Eng.)

Director, James Watt Nanofabrication Centre

Dr Martin Weides is a Professor of Quantum Technologies at the University of Glasgow, leading the Quantum Circuits group. His research focuses on coherent nanoelectronics, quantum spintronics, and quantum computing with superconducting circuits.

Prof. Hiu Yung Wong

Professor of Electrical Engineering (SJSU)

Silicon Valley AMDT Endowed Chair

Hiu Yung Wong brings 12+ years of industry experience from Synopsys and Spansion. A Ph.D. graduate from UC Berkeley, his work spans quantum computing, machine learning, and device simulation, having received the NSF CAREER award.

Dr. Abuzer Yakaryilmaz

Assistant Professor at University of Latvia

Co-Founder of QWorld Association

Dr Yakaryilmaz combines quantum automata and complexity theory with a strong commitment to quantum education. Founder of QWorld, a global non-profit association training the next generation of quantum developers across 25+ countries.

Sasha Lazarevic

IBM Senior AI Architect

IBM Quantum Ambassador

Sasha is a technology leader at IBM with over 23 years of experience. A long-standing IBM Quantum Ambassador and Qiskit Advocate, he specializes in quantum algorithms, machine learning, and quantum finance.

Jim McKay

IBM Cloud Solutions Architect

Enterprise Architecture Specialist

Jim McKay is a Solution Architect at IBM Cloud with over 24 years of experience. He is a subject matter expert in cloud architecture, translating complex enterprise compute systems to hybrid and quantum-ready cloud environments.
