Confirmed Speakers

Dr Sarah Croke

Professor of Theoretical Physics

School of Physics and Astronomy, University of Glasgow

Sarah Croke is a Professor of Theoretical Physics in the Quantum Theory Group at the University of Glasgow. After a BSc in Physics and Applied Mathematics at University College Cork and an MSc in Astrophysics, she completed her PhD in quantum information and quantum optics at the University of Strathclyde in 2007 under Prof Stephen Barnett. She then spent nearly six years at the Perimeter Institute in Waterloo, Canada, first as a postdoctoral fellow and later as a PSI fellow, before joining Glasgow in 2013. Her research centres on quantum measurement and quantum information, particularly protocols that need minimal quantum processing and are feasible with near-term technology, with recent work including a quantum algorithm for gravitational-wave matched filtering.

Dr Ashley Lyons

Senior Lecturer, University of Glasgow

Royal Academy of Engineering Research Fellow

Ashley Lyons is a Senior Lecturer and Royal Academy of Engineering Research Fellow in the School of Physics and Astronomy at the University of Glasgow, based in the Advanced Research Centre as part of the Extreme Light group. He completed his PhD at Heriot-Watt University in 2017, joined Glasgow as a Research Associate in 2018 and became a Lecturer in 2020, and was awarded a Leverhulme Trust Early Career Fellowship in 2021. His research develops new forms of single-photon time-of-flight sensing, combining them with machine learning and quantum effects such as entanglement and two-photon interference for applications including LiDAR, microscopy and imaging through biological tissue. He also co-leads the Scottish Funding Council's Alliance for Research Challenges in Quantum Technology.

Mingqi Zhang

Research Assistant, Quantum Information Hardware Lab

University of Glasgow

Mingqi Zhang is a Research Assistant at the Quantum Information Hardware Lab at the University of Glasgow, working with Dr Kaveh Delfanazari. Her research focuses on continuous-variable quantum key distribution at microwave, millimetre-wave and terahertz frequencies, with work published in IEEE Transactions on Quantum Engineering on secure wireless quantum links for applications from superconducting quantum computers to inter-satellite communication. She also contributes to superconducting quantum hardware research, including on-chip sensing in tantalum superconducting circuits. In her talk, she will share her experience as a researcher.

Kiran Kaur Raina (Natashia)

Founder & CEO of NucleQi

Quantum Technology Educator & Researcher

Kiran Kaur Raina, known as Natashia, is the founder and CEO of the quantum education startup NucleQi and a Quantum Security Research Engineer & Evangelist at Vyapti Resonance, alongside her studies at IIT Madras. Her research spans quantum machine learning and quantum cryptography, and she serves as a brand ambassador for Classiq Technologies. Since launching her YouTube channel in late 2023, she has grown an audience of 66,000+ followers by making complex quantum ideas accessible, earning international award recognition and a feature on a Times Square billboard in New York City. As a Quantum Pioneer Legacy Initiative mentor, she supports young women and girls aspiring to become the next generation of quantum leaders.

Júlia Santos

Quantum Machine Learning Researcher

Qiskit Advocate

Júlia Santos is a Computer Engineering student at FIAP in Brazil, a quantum computing researcher and a Qiskit Advocate. Her research focuses on quantum machine learning and deep learning for medical imaging, including a study comparing quantum transfer learning models against classical CNNs for breast cancer classification. Working across Qiskit, PyTorch and PennyLane, she brings software engineering rigour to quantum research through MLOps, CI/CD, automated testing and reproducible experiment tracking. She has also benchmarked quantum optimisers against classical baselines on protein folding as part of a hackathon run by Qollab with IonQ.
